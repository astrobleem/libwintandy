WINDOWS PSG BANK INTEGRATION

The player offers Default and Chamber from the MIDI bank button. It saves
its choice in app-local WINAMP.INI, [FauxBlaster] Bank=0 or1. Compact mode
uses the short Default/Chamber caption. Setup and normal GO stay unchanged.
The shared SOUND.DRV remains the same9752-byte provider.

MDInstrumentBank(handle,bank), ordinal19, validates task ownership and0/1.
MDSeedBank(handle,generation,time,message,voiceBank), ordinal20, restores a
held voice with its latched bank, then restores the explicit selection.
MciBank(device,bank), ordinal905, is the file-player setter. CC0/32 and
MDVoiceMode retain their existing meanings; they are not bank controls.

MDOpen starts Default. Generation/reset paths reapply the selection to both
synth and folded. The app-local live renderer does so on open/reset/close;
its held voices seed their own bank during reconstruction. New melodic
notes latch the current bank; existing and pedal-held voices keep theirs.
Percussion, original MIDI note keys, controllers and speaker routing retain
their existing behavior. The canonical bank input is libtandy c5fbc45.

The native mixed-bank test exposed a paired-live guard bug: HiStatus is the
broker state, not a Boolean error.1 is armed and2 running. The old guard
rejected all nonzero states, so every second update on a running paired
clock failed. Its host mock incorrectly returned0 while running. The fixed
guard accepts owned1/2 with a callback, requires idle0 without a callback
when inactive, and retains paused/stopping/detached/foreign refusals. It
checks and replaces state in one IRQ-masked transaction. There is no new
timer, owner, driver or unsafe restart/chaining path.

Native MSC6 /G0 /Gw /Gs /AS /W3 /Od; Windows3.0 real mode,8086_prefetch,
normal640KB; no XMS/EMS/UMB or FPU. Recorded segment minima in bytes:
  RAWCLK: CODE13867 DGROUP27168 (CODE+67; data unchanged)
  MIDICLK: CODE14844 DGROUP12148 (CODE+492/data+130)
  MCITNDY: CODE11192 DGROUP3404 (CODE+244/data+6)
  MINIPLAY: CODE48202 DGROUP16016 (CODE+688/data+416)
Native TVOICE is10bytes and MSYN332; bank fields use existing padding.
The player retains2048-byte heap and8192-byte stack allocations.
