libwintandy audio facade - Windows3.0 source candidate

LWAUDIO.H forwards the real R4 MCIAPI.H and restored guarded RAWAPP.H.
MINIPLAY's app-local MCITNDY path still feeds MIDICLK and RAWCLK; its TND/AGI
path still uses RAWAPP/RAWFILE and RAWCLK. API names, export ordinals, packed
messages, generation and timestamp semantics are unchanged. MDAPI.H and
TIMECLK.H remain the advanced provider contracts. No new expressive patch
format, live callback, mixer, owner task or scheduler is introduced.

The R4 raw-score utility collided with the player's RAWAPP.H name. This source
candidate restores the exact R3 declarations with guards and assigns guarded
RAWSCORE.H to the utility. Five raw-port include changes remain in the isolated
complete-source worktree. Their musical/data content is not bundled here.
Fresh player/RAWQA/five-port builds are required before binary publication.
Historical R4 tests apply to their exact recorded binaries, not a fresh build
from the defective historical header. Provider/IRQ/ownership bodies are intact.

Retain PIT-cycle raw timestamps, full-group commits, generation checks and
accepted-minus-delivered pending accounting. FULL accepts nothing: retain/retry
the exact suffix. EOF means actual drain and terminal silence. Pause retains
owner/queue and freezes/mutes; resume continues. Acknowledged Reset precedes
stop/restart/refill; successful Close precedes unload/destruction. BUSY retains
handles/modules for retry. Use genuine FAR buffers and audited DS/SS handling.
No parsing, allocation, Windows/DOS calls or client callback belongs in IRQ.

The facade exposes the existing semantics without promising universal 1ms or
live-key latency. Stage5 instrument steps remain55ms and prepared-frame minimum
spacing remains8ms. No AY hardware envelopes, sampled vocals or RPI evaluator
are added. The app-local MCI module is not a full MMSYSTEM implementation.

UI integration does not apply text suppression to MINIPLAY's custom controls;
their GDI painting, clipping, playlist and invalidation bodies remain unchanged.
